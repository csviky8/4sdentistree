01 / DENTAL CARE

Prosthodontic teeth replacement

A Fixed Partial Denture, commonly known as a bridge, is designed to replace missing teeth by relying on the support of neighboring teeth, which may require a reduction of tooth structure. This method is often preferred for its affordability compared to dental implants. While discussing options, it's essential to consider various treatments, including pediatric orthodontics for younger patients, root canal therapy when necessary, and veneer treatment for aesthetic enhancements.

02 / DENTAL CARE

Early Childhood caries

Restoring early childhood caries is crucial as this represents an active infection in your child's mouth. If left untreated, it can impact the formation of adult teeth within the bone. Neglecting caries can lead to tooth loss, resulting in less space for permanent teeth to erupt. To address these issues, options such as affordable orthodontics, dental implants for missing teeth, and pediatric orthodontics are available. In some cases, root canal therapy may be necessary, and veneer treatment can enhance the appearance of affected teeth.

03 / DENTAL CARE

Dental crowns

Teeth that have undergone root canal therapy require a crown to restore their strength. During this procedure, the pulp that contains blood supply and nerves is removed, as decay can weaken the tooth structure. A crown or cap made of metal, ceramic, or a combination of both offers vital support for the teeth. Additionally, for those considering affordable orthodontics solutions or dental implants, it's important to maintain a strong foundation through effective treatments like veneer treatment and pediatric orthodontics.

Discuss your treatment ↗
04 / DENTAL CARE

Veneers

Veneer treatment is an ideal option to address mild spaces between teeth, especially when teeth are discolored or abnormal in shape. For those seeking solutions, affordable orthodontics and dental implants are also available, along with pediatric orthodontics for younger patients. Additionally, root canal therapy may be required in some cases for comprehensive dental care.

Discuss your treatment ↗
05 / DENTAL CARE

Dental Implants

Dental implants are one of the most ideal options for replacing a missing tooth. They hold the alveolar bone, preventing bone loss from the site, and importantly, they don't require support from neighboring teeth. For those seeking cost-effective solutions, affordable orthodontics can also complement treatments like veneer treatment and pediatric orthodontics, while root canal therapy may be considered in preserving existing teeth.

06 / DENTAL CARE

Pediatric crowns

Carious baby teeth need to be restored immediately; if the infection is left untreated, it can affect the developing permanent teeth. Seeking affordable orthodontics or pediatric orthodontics can help ensure proper care, and in cases where infection is severe, root canal therapy or dental implants might be necessary. Additionally, veneer treatment can enhance the appearance of teeth once they are restored.

07 / DENTAL CARE

Knocked out teeth

Do not fret if you knock off your teeth; it's important to meet your dentist immediately. If your tooth is completely knocked out, take it to the dentist by storing it in milk, coconut water, or your saliva. When faced with such emergencies, consulting a professional, whether for affordable orthodontics or dental implants, is crucial. Avoid washing the tooth in tap water or wiping it with tissue. If you're a parent considering pediatric orthodontics for your child, keeping their dental health in mind is essential, especially after accidents. Remember, waiting too long can lead to the need for root canal therapy or even veneer treatment, so act quickly.

08 / DENTAL CARE

Occlusal splint

An occlusal splint can help prevent damage to the structures of teeth in individuals who grind their teeth at night, which is an important consideration for those seeking affordable orthodontics or preparing for dental implants. Additionally, pediatric orthodontics can address early signs of grinding, while root canal therapy and veneer treatment can be relevant interventions for any resulting damage.

09 / DENTAL CARE

Tooth Decay

Ignoring a cavity or decay in your teeth can lead to the infection spreading to the pulp, resulting in severe nighttime pain and sensitivity to hot and cold foods. At this critical stage, treatment typically involves root canal therapy to remove the infected pulp. For patients seeking comprehensive care, including options like affordable orthodontics or pediatric orthodontics, it’s essential to address these issues promptly to prevent further complications. Additionally, once the infection is treated, dental implants or veneer treatment may be considered to restore the tooth’s function and aesthetics.

10 / DENTAL CARE

Root Canal Therapy

The teeth need to be maintained with proper oral hygiene, even after root canal therapy, to prevent secondary infection. Sometimes, even teeth previously treated with root canal will require a new Re-RCT to eliminate this infection that could spread beyond the tooth roots. It's also essential to consider affordable orthodontics and pediatric orthodontics options for younger patients, as well as looking into dental implants and veneer treatment for comprehensive dental care.

11 / DENTAL CARE

Implant crown

After dental implants are placed in the alveolar bone, they undergo a healing phase lasting 3-6 months. Following this period, the implant is restored with an implant crown or cap, a process that is often part of broader treatments like affordable orthodontics or veneer treatment. Additionally, some patients may also require pediatric orthodontics or root canal therapy before restorative procedures.

12 / DENTAL CARE

Complete Denture

Complete dentures are custom-made removable prosthetic devices that replace all of your missing teeth in either the upper or lower jaw, providing a natural-looking smile and restoring essential functions like chewing and speaking. Crafted to fit the unique contours of your mouth, these dentures offer comfort and support while enhancing your overall oral health. For those seeking affordable orthodontics, dental implants may be an option to consider for a more permanent solution. Additionally, pediatric orthodontics can help children develop proper alignment early on. For adults, treatments like root canal therapy or veneer treatment can further improve dental aesthetics and function.

14 / DENTAL CARE

Impacted wisdom teeth

The third molar, commonly known as wisdom teeth or the 8th teeth, often gets impacted and fails to erupt into the oral cavity. These teeth are frequently malpositioned, leading to food accumulation behind the last molar or causing gum pain. For those seeking solutions like affordable orthodontics or pediatric orthodontics, the removal of wisdom teeth can be an effective option for managing impacted teeth. Additionally, for individuals interested in dental implants, addressing issues with wisdom teeth can improve overall oral health before considering advanced treatments like veneer treatment or root canal therapy.

15 / DENTAL CARE

Extraction

Teeth that cannot be saved or are damaged due to fracture or caries beyond repair need to be extracted. After extraction, it is crucial to replace the tooth in order to maintain oral health. Options such as dental implants can provide a permanent solution, while affordable orthodontics and pediatric orthodontics are available for younger patients to ensure proper alignment. In some cases, if the tooth is salvageable, root canal therapy might be an option instead of extraction. Additionally, for cosmetic enhancements, veneer treatment can improve the appearance of the surrounding teeth.
