OUR FOUNDER

A passion for
the possible.

Dr. Swapna Sreenivasagan

MDS (Orthodontics), PhD · Chief Dental Surgeon

I grew up immersed in fantasy and fiction, believing wholeheartedly in magic. My course in life changed dramatically when I discovered Dentistry. As a young student, I watched in awe as Orthodontists showcased the amazing transformations of their patients, and I longed to wield that magical touch. I completed my postgraduate studies and doctorate at Saveetha Dental College, realizing that it was not a magic wand but rather dedicated hard work, grit, determination, and countless hours of effort that brought about the real magic in dental services.

I don't want to sound mystical, but when I see the beautiful smile created through orthodontics, especially with the use of innovative solutions like invisible aligners, something truly powerful takes over. That’s the essence of Dentistry.

OUR MISSION

Healthy smiles.
Happier lives.

At 4S Dentistree, our goal is to provide exceptional dental services to our patients. We believe that a healthy smile is the foundation of a happy life, and through our advanced orthodontics, including invisible aligners, we are dedicated to helping our patients achieve and maintain optimal oral health.

We offer a full range of dental services for the whole family, including routine checkups, teeth cleaning, fillings, extractions, and orthodontics. Our goal is to help you maintain a healthy and beautiful smile.
